The Photo Comes With It

A new Excel export puts every item’s photo in the row beside its description. Plus quoted amount and booked status on every survey, clearer statistics, and unused links that refund their credit.

The Manual Step That’s Gone

Your Inventory, Photos Included

Here is how one international mover was building his working spreadsheet before this release. Open the walkthrough video. Pause it. Screenshot the item. Paste the screenshot into a sheet. Type the description next to it. Move to the next item. Repeat, for every survey.

His team runs one spreadsheet for two jobs: reviewing insurance valuations with the customer, and handing a packing list to the crew with notes on anything that needs special handling. Both jobs need the picture and the description side by side. The product had both. It just had no way to hand them over together.

Now it does. Open … More and, under Export as, next to CSV, there is Excel (with photos). The file has one row per item with the item’s photo embedded in the row, beside its description, quantities, volume, weight and tags.

No pausing the video.

No screenshotting your own survey.

The photo is already in the row.

Excel sits next to CSV under Export as.

One row per item, photo included, opened straight from the export.

Fourteen columns, in this order: Room, Item, Photo, Category, Qty Ship, Qty Excl, Volume, Weight, Tags, In Carton, Pro-Gear, Reviewed, Flagged, Notes. The header row comes with filters, so narrowing to one room or one tag is a click, not a formula. Quantities, volume and weight are real numbers, so a column total just works.

Export CSV has not changed. It is still there, still the same file. A CSV is plain text and cannot hold a picture, so Excel sits next to it as a second option. If your workflow runs on the CSV, nothing about it moved.

After the Review

Did It Book?

A survey used to end at the inventory. What happened next, what you quoted and whether they went with you, lived in somebody’s head or in another system. Standalone now keeps it on the survey itself: Quoted Amount, Move Date, Booked and Booked Date.

Move Date can be set right at the start, alongside the customer’s name and email when you generate the survey link. It is optional if you don’t know it yet.

Afterwards, the survey row’s Actions menu opens Edit Info, where quoted amount, move date and booked status can be added or changed. Booked Date fills itself in the moment you tick Booked.

Quoted amount, move date and booked status, on the survey instead of in someone's head.

The same fields appear in the Review Complete window, right where you download or email the PDF. Fill them in if you have them, skip them if you don’t.

Log the outcome at the moment you send the report.

Note: survey outcome fields are in HomeSurvey.ai standalone. The Movegistics CRM already has its own flows for move date, quoted amount and booking.
