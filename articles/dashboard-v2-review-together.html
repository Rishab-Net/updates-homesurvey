Review Together

Put Your Customer in the Room

Some conversations don’t work over a phone call and a PDF. Review Together lets you invite the customer into a live co-review session: they open a link, and they’re looking at the same survey you are — with a video call built right in. No third-party meeting app, no screen-share fiddling.

As you navigate, follow-me navigation keeps their view on yours — open the garage, they see the garage. Every edit you make syncs to their screen the moment you make it: “the treadmill isn’t coming? Watch, I’m taking it off right now.” The customer’s view is read-only, so they can see everything and change nothing. Works on desktop and mobile, on both sides.
