Improvement Customer-Driven November 24, 2026

The Customer Said It. The Survey Counts It.

Our latest release changes how an AI survey treats what your customer tells you, and how it handles items that should not be counted twice or should not ship at all.

AI Surveys

What Changed

The customer’s words decide what ships.

If a customer is not sure about an item, or is trying to sell it, it shows on its own line and stays out of the confirmed total. A count the customer says out loud is the count the survey uses.

What the customer types counts too.

A note typed in the app, such as “Fridge stays”, now works like a spoken one.

One item, counted once.

An item seen from two rooms, or a room uploaded twice, is counted once.

Built-ins are marked, not shipped.

A kitchen island or a closet’s own shelving is set aside with a note to confirm with the customer.

What You Will See

Items the customer is unsure about sit on their own line. Rows the survey set aside carry a short note saying why, so your estimator can confirm with the customer in one call.
